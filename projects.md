An overview of the things I've built over the years, grouped roughly by category. This page is a summary rather than a project-by-project archive — ongoing updates and new write-ups show up as posts on the [home page](/).

## RC Multicopters & FPV

Most of my build history lives here: a string of custom FPV quads going back to 2015 — Zax, Tangra, an X-frame racer, SiganX, KISS X210/QAV210, Shrieka 130, and others — along with the usual side quests that come with the hobby: flight-controller tuning on Cleanflight/Betaflight, motor and ESC thrust testing, FPV camera comparisons, and eventually getting a HAM radio license (KK6VYN) to run higher-power video gear legally.

## DIY / Maker Projects

Practical home projects, usually involving a microcontroller and more 3D-printed parts than strictly necessary. Highlights include a Smart Pool Thermometer (ESP32 + Prometheus + Grafana for remote monitoring), a yard speaker rebuild (Raspberry Pi, Bluetooth, a proper amp, and a Fusion 360-designed enclosure), and getting an Ender 3 up and running — which then fed straight back into printing parts for the drone builds above.

## Software Projects

Smaller tools and services written to support the hardware projects, mostly embedded and IoT-adjacent: ESP32-based sensor and lighting controllers, home-monitoring dashboards, and the occasional off-topic experiment. Most of it lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/jaisor).
